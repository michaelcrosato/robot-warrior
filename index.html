First contact: Press B to move, then R to select a target. Aim at its box. Hold the aim steady for missile lock. Switch weapons while they recharge. At 100% heat, the reactor shuts down. Press G to cool it.

Extended mission: Destroy both basin sites and all basin defenders. Cross NAV CHARLIE. Cut both power feeds at Ashfall Works, then destroy the exposed reactor. Cross Cinder Cut at NAV GOLF. Destroy the Skyguard battery at NAV HOTEL. Secure the flight-control codes at NAV INDIA. Then reach NAV JULIET for extraction. Stop inside each blue repair bay for 6 seconds to repair and rearm once.

Flight-control link: Stop inside the cyan ring at NAV INDIA. Stay on the ground for a total of 40 seconds. You can turn and fire. Leaving the ring, moving, reactor shutdown, or enemies within 90 meters will pause the transfer. Your progress is saved. Defeat both counterattack waves to open extraction. The flight beacon cannot be destroyed.

Enhanced Imaging [I]: Shows real mesh edges. Terrain is green. Objectives are blue. Enemy armor is amber; damaged parts turn red. Solid cover still blocks the view and weapons. Press I again for the normal view. Night vision [V] remains separate.

Online co-op: Select ONLINE CO-OP. The host creates a room and shares its code. Each pilot chooses a mech and selects READY. The host deploys the team. Armor, ammunition, and one use of each repair bay are separate for each pilot. Mission objectives and score are shared. Friendly fire is off.

Teammate recovery: Stop within 32 meters of a downed pilot. Hold J for 6 seconds to restore the mech. One pilot can hold the flight-link ring while the others defend it. All living pilots must reach extraction. The mission fails if all pilots are down.

Local menus: Esc opens your menu, but the online mission continues. Your mech stops and remains vulnerable. If the host hides the game tab, the team is put on hold until the host resumes. The host must keep the game open. Host migration and joining after deployment are not supported.

Original game code, art, sound effects, and radio prompts. Mission music is an optional local asset and is not distributed with this build: without it, the game plays normally and music is silent. When present, a startup cue plays at boot, one track loops over Kestrel Basin, and a second loops once the basin is clear. In solo play, pause stops playback and resume continues from the same point. Online music follows the shared mission stage. Inspired by 1990s heavy robot simulations. Not affiliated with MechWarrior or its rights holders. Solo play uses no network. Online play uses external signaling and ICE services. WebRTC signaling follows the PeerJS 1.5.5 protocol. No camera or microphone is used.

NETWORK SETTINGS & CONNECTION HELP

Online play uses WebRTC and a public PeerServer to connect players. No account, camera, or microphone is required. Public services can be unavailable. A private server or TURN relay can be set below. The optional server kit supports WebSocket relay mode when direct connections fail.

Relay credentials stay in this page. They are not put in invites. Use the same server settings on each computer. A room code is not a password: share it only with your friends.
